PostSet

A static prototype covering every story in the backlog except Story 4 (Won't). Four screens, each with its own subscreens: sheets, tabs and states. Nothing saves and nothing plays.

Start at Shows and tap through, or open any subscreen below. Each one has its own link, like song.html#angles. On a phone, each screen fills the display.
