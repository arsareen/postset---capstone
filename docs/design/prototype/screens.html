PostSet

A static prototype covering every story in the backlog except Story 4 (Won't). It builds on postset.app's look and adds new ideas: multiview, a crowd map made of footage, a setlist coverage strip, a "phone down" moment after recording, and a tap-the-crowd "Where were you?".

Four screens, each with its own subscreens. Every subscreen has its own link, like song.html#multiview. Nothing saves and nothing plays.
